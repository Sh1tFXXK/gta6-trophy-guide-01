    The road to the platinum

    One playthrough is enough. There are no difficulty settings, no online trophies and no mission replay, so play the story first and clean up in free roam afterwards. The plan below follows PowerPyx’s three-step roadmap and the official descriptions: which chapters, which missions, which collectibles and how many of each.

      Before you start

      • Keep cheats off the save you use for the platinum: guides report that cheats disable trophies and stop the game saving.
      • There is no mission replay, so keep a manual save at the start and end of every chapter. The game has 15 manual slots plus one autosave.
      • Progress made during the story already counts toward the 100% checklist, even though the checklist itself only appears after the story, under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion.

      Sources: PowerPyx trophy guide & 100% completion checklist; Sixshore trophy list.

      Missable trophies

      Built from the official descriptions and the guides that have played the game: only one trophy is reported as possibly missable. Everything else can still be earned in free roam after the story.

      Looks missable, but is not

      • Story trophies: all 12 pop automatically as you play; none can be missed.
      • Stranger strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret): quest givers who go quiet mid-story all return and stay after the ending.
      • Optional missions with an hourglass icon can expire, but none of them is tied to a trophy.
      • Some fences leave for good; none of them has a trophy quest.
      • Jason and Lucia’s relationship does not matter for any trophy.

      Sources: PowerPyx trophy guide; Sixshore trophy list.

        How the tracker works

        • Tick a trophy to mark it earned. Progress is saved in this browser’s local storage.
        • Export downloads your progress as a JSON file; Import reads one back.
        • The spoiler setting hides the names and descriptions of story trophies until you earn them. Hidden trophies on PlayStation (28 of the 55) are marked.
        • Kinds: story (12), missable (1), collectibles, activities. There are no online trophies in this game, so the Online filter is empty on purpose.

        What this guide is not

        It uses no Rockstar logo, artwork, box art or game screenshots. The look is our own. It fetches nothing at run time and works with the network off.
